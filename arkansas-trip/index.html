Gaps

What isn't booked, ordered by how much it would hurt to leave alone.

Do first

A car

Nothing found in email. The airport is a half-hour out, and the weekend has you moving between town, the museum and trailheads — this is the one gap that quietly breaks the rest of the plan if it stays open. Late September is not the month to leave it to the counter.

Do first

A plan B for Salt Lake

Forty-two minutes is a legal connection and nothing more. If the San Francisco leg pushes back at all, you get rebooked onto a later Salt Lake–XNA flight and Thursday evening disappears. Worth knowing what else Delta runs into XNA that afternoon before you're standing in a terminal finding out.

Soon

Thursday's table

The only night without one. A 7:30 booking made now costs nothing and saves three hungry men negotiating on a pavement.

Soon

Three bikes for Saturday

If riding is the reason to come to Bentonville at all, walking into a shop on a peak-season Saturday morning is optimistic. The Square has rental outfits doing trail bikes and e-bikes both.

Check

Whether Crystal Bridges has a ticketed show

Walking into the permanent collection is free, but whatever headline exhibition is running in September will be ticketed and can sell out. Worth a look closer to the date.

Confirm

When Paul and Steve actually land

Only James's booking is in this inbox. Everything on Thursday hangs on the latest of the three arrivals, so that's worth pinning down before a table gets booked around it.

Nice to have

Something for Sunday

Eleven o'clock checkout, six o'clock flight. Five and a half hours is a long time to improvise with three sets of luggage.
